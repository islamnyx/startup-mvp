# Value Proposition

One-liner: "Let your AI agents work on real systems without giving them the keys to everything, prove what they did, and prove who authorized it."

Three outcomes:
- Control (permissions, MCP governance, approvals, instant revocation)
- Proof (tamper-evident audit trail with delegation chains for regulators and executives)
- Speed to adoption (security teams say yes to agent projects they would otherwise block)

The third is the strongest: selling the thing that unblocks their AI roadmap.
