# Business Model

## Core
Annual subscription, priced per agent or per environment, tiers by governance features.

## Services
Paid pilots, integration, security assessments. In early stages this is most of the revenue, and that's fine.

## Enterprise
Private or on-premise deployment priced as a premium.

## Later
Usage fees, certifications, training.

## Algeria issue
Selling SaaS to local companies is complicated by payment channels, foreign-currency limits, and companies preferring local invoicing and contracts. Plan for DZD contracts and invoicing from day one. International customers easier to bill but harder to reach.
