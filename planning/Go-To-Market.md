# Go-To-Market

1. Credibility first: publish red-team demos and write-ups (agent attacked, policy blocks it, full audit trail shown). CTF and security community reach is a real asset.
2. Design partners: 2-3 organizations, heavily discounted or free pilot in exchange for feedback and a reference (focus on MCP-heavy agent deployments).
3. Paid pilot to contract: convert one pilot into a real contract and case study.
4. Channel partners: local integrators, consultancies, system vendors who already have the customer relationship and can resell.
5. Then expand to nearby francophone and North African markets (similar language and regulation).
