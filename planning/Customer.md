# Customer

## Economic buyer
CIO, CISO, head of risk or compliance. They sign the check.

## User
The IT or automation team building the agents.

## Champion
Usually an engineer or security person who has already felt the problem.

## Segments (most to least promising)
1. [[Regulated Orgs]] — banks, insurers, telcos, energy. Large budgets, real fear, slow sales cycles.
2. [[Tech Companies]] — faster to close, smaller checks, more technical buyers.
3. [[Mid-Size Businesses]] — largest pool, but buy on savings not security. Weakest fit.
