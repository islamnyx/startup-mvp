# Competition

Big platforms (Microsoft, Google, AWS) and agent frameworks will add their own guardrails, but they're not neutral: they govern their agents. Your opening is being vendor-neutral and locally trusted.

## Realistic moats
- Trust and relationships in home market
- Compliance knowledge (local data protection rules, sector regulations)
- Ability to deploy inside the customer's own infrastructure
- Track record of red-team results proving the product works

None of these is a hard technical moat. This is a trust-and-distribution business more than a technology monopoly.
