# Phases

| Phase | Goal | Success signal |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 months | Customer discovery, 15-20 interviews | Clear picture of who has the pain and budget |
| 3-9 months | Prototype plus 2-3 design partners | Partners actively using it |
| 9-18 months | First paid contracts, one reference case | Revenue and a customer who'll vouch |
| 18+ months | Repeatable sales, second market | Pipeline not dependent on founders' networks |
