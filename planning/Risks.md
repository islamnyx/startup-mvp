# Risks

- Market timing: if Algerian companies aren't deploying agents yet, selling a solution before the problem exists.
- Long sales cycles in the segments that care most about security, straining a student team's runway.
- Overbuilding before a single customer says "I'd pay for this."
- Founder bandwidth: studies, startup, and products ([[Products]]) competing for the same hours. Focus on one is a business decision.
- Legal and funding structure: check Algerian startup status, incubator terms, IP ownership early; verify current details with the incubator directly.
