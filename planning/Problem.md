# Problem

Companies are letting AI agents read data and take actions (send emails, update records, approve things), and nobody can answer three questions:
- What can this agent touch?
- What did it actually do?
- Who is accountable if it goes wrong?

With MCP adoption exploding, agents are connecting to more systems than ever - without governance. The pain is a **fear of loss** (data leaks, fraud, regulatory trouble), which is a different sale from "saves you hours." They also need to **prove delegation** - who authorized each action - for audits and compliance.
